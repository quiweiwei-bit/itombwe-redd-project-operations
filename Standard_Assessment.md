This document provides a comparative assessment of two carbon certification standards for the Itombwe REDD+ project:

| **Standard** | **Methodology** | **Status** |

| **Equitable Earth** | M002 (REDD+ AUDD) | Available; DRC risk maps ready |

## 2. Equitable Earth Assessment

### 2.1 Overview

Equitable Earth is a next-generation carbon certification standard exclusively focused on nature-based solutions. The M002 methodology for REDD+ AUDD projects was launched at COP30 in November 2025.

**Key Differentiators:**
- Digital-first platform
- Three non-negotiable pillars: carbon, livelihoods, ecological condition
- Centralised carbon accounting
- ICVCM and EBA approved

### 2.2 Methodology: M002

The M002 methodology quantifies emission reductions from activities that avoid unplanned deforestation and/or forest degradation (AUDD).

**Key Features:**
- Uses advanced remote sensing and machine learning
- Monitors quantitative changes in Above-Ground Biomass (AGB)
- Project outcomes independently verified annually using latest satellite data
- Risk maps updated annually
- Baselines updated every five years

### 2.3 DRC Risk Map Status

**Status:** ✅ **Already available**

Equitable Earth has already developed risk maps for the DRC. These are updated annually using the latest satellite, biomass, and land cover data.

**Implication:** The project can proceed immediately without waiting for risk maps.

### 2.6 Market Recognition

- Accepted by coalitions involving **Microsoft, Google, Meta, Salesforce, and Netflix**
- Rated **3 out of 10** by Sylvera (compared to Verra's VM047 which received 6 out of 10)
- Working toward **ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (CCP) approval** for M002 by end of 2026

### 2.7 Three Pillars (All Non-Negotiable)

| **Pillar** | **What is Assessed** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Carbon** | Emissions reductions; baseline allocation via jurisdictional risk maps |
| **Ecological Condition** | Physical composition, species composition, structural diversity, ecosystem function |
| **Livelihoods** | Community benefits; FPIC; revenue-sharing; grievance mechanisms |

### 2.8 Project Eligibility

**Non-Negotiable Requirements:**
1. Land tenure must be secure and verifiable
2. FPIC must be documented and genuine
3. Carbon, livelihoods, and ecological condition pillars must be met
4. Project must demonstrate additionality

**"Visibility Phase":** Equitable Earth conducts early eligibility checks before formal registry inclusion. Projects that fail these checks are rejected early to protect the registry's reputation.

## 3. Verra Assessment

### 3.1 Overview

Verra is the world's largest voluntary carbon market registry, with over 1,900 projects and approximately 1 billion credits issued. The VM0048 methodology is the established standard for REDD+ projects.

**Key Differentiators:**
- Global market leader
- Highest buyer recognition and confidence
- Established methodologies and processes

### 3.2 Methodology: VM0048

VM0048 is the REDD+ methodology under the Verified Carbon Standard (VCS) programme.

**Key Features:**
- Uses VMD0055-compliant allocated deforestation risk maps
- Projects must acquire risk maps via Verra Project Hub
- Dynamic baselines
- 30-year crediting period typical

### 3.3 DRC Risk Map Status

**Status:** ⚠️ **Itombwe/South Kivu NOT yet published**

Verra released final deforestation risk maps for **Mai Ndombe** in May 2026. However, Itombwe (South Kivu) is **NOT listed** on Verra's public timetable.

**The Critical Bottleneck:**
- Projects **must** have VMD0055-compliant risk maps to proceed
- Without these maps, **no baseline can be set**
- **No registration can occur**
- The waiting period is **unknown** — could be 6–24+ months

**Verra's Own Statement:** "These delays have contributed to broader frustration among REDD+ project developers, who face bottlenecks across registration, validation and verification processes."

### 3.6 Market Recognition

- **Global market leader** — over 1,900 projects
- Credits typically trade at **10–40% premium** over unknown registries
- Highest buyer confidence and recognition
- Widely accepted by corporate and compliance buyers

### 3.7 VCS v5.0 Transition

**Critical Deadline:** If your initial registration request is submitted **after 1 January 2027**, you **must** use VCS Version 5.

- Projects with start dates before 1 January 2027 may apply certain Version 4 requirements
- Transition adds complexity and potential delays
- Version 5 requirements are not yet fully operationalised
