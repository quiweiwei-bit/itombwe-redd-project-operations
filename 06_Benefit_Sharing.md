# Benefit Sharing Plan: Itombwe REDD+ Project

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 31 July 2026
**Prepared By:** Congo Rainforest Fund (CRF)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the benefit-sharing framework for the Itombwe REDD+ project. Benefit sharing is a requirement under:
- DRC Ministerial Order No. 047
- Equitable Earth certification standards
- Verra VCS safeguards requirements
- UNFCCC REDD+ safeguards

---

## 2. Benefit Sharing: Key Principles

| **Principle** | **Description** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Transparency** | All benefit-sharing arrangements must be transparent and clear |
| **Equity** | Benefits must be distributed fairly among all stakeholders |
| **Inclusivity** | All community members must benefit, including marginalised groups |
| **Accountability** | Benefit distribution must be monitored and reported |
| **Sustainability** | Benefits must support long-term community development |

---

## 3. DRC Legal Requirements for Benefit Sharing

### 3.1 Ministerial Order No. 047

- Requires REDD+ project developers to **negotiate benefit-sharing agreements** with stakeholders
- Does NOT provide guidance on evaluating or enforcing these agreements

### 3.2 Interministerial Order No. 006/120 (September 2023)

- Specifies allocation of national carbon revenue quotas
- **Does NOT specify a minimum share for Indigenous Peoples and Local Communities**
- Up to **15% of authorised emission reductions** allocated as share of proceeds for national climate priorities

### 3.3 2022 Indigenous Pygmy Law

- Recognises Indigenous Peoples' right to benefit from ecosystem services on their territories
- Provides a legal basis for benefit-sharing with Indigenous communities

**Implication:** Your benefit-sharing plan should go beyond minimum legal requirements.

## 4. Benefit Sharing Framework

### 4.1 Categories of Benefits

| **Category** | **Description** | **Examples** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial Benefits** | Direct payments to communities | Cash payments; community development funds |
| **Livelihood Benefits** | Income-generating activities | Alternative livelihoods; sustainable agriculture |
| **Capacity Building** | Training and education | Conservation training; business skills |
| **Infrastructure** | Community facilities | Schools; health centres; water systems |
| **Natural Resource Benefits** | Sustainable resource use | Sustainable harvesting; community forests |

### 4.2 Eligible Beneficiaries

| **Group** | **Inclusion Criteria** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Local Communities** | All community members living in project area |
| **Indigenous Peoples** | Pygmy and other Indigenous groups |
| **Traditional Authorities** | Chiefs and traditional leaders |
| **Women** | Women's groups and female community members |
| **Youth** | Young community members |
| **Vulnerable Groups** | Marginalised and disadvantaged groups |

### 4.3 Benefit Distribution Mechanism

| **Mechanism** | **Description** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Community Fund** | Percentage of carbon revenue allocated to community development fund |
| **Direct Payments** | Direct cash payments to eligible community members |
| **Project Activities** | Community-driven project activities (e.g., patrols, monitoring) |
| **Capacity Building** | Training and education programmes |
| **Infrastructure** | Community-selected infrastructure projects |

### 4.4 Benefit Sharing Structure (Suggested)

| **Beneficiary** | **Suggested Share** | **Notes** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Local Communities** | 40–50% | Primary beneficiaries |
| **ICCN / Protected Area Management** | 10–20% | For ongoing management costs |
| **DRC Government** | 0–15% | As required by law |
| **Project Management** | 20–30% | For project operations and sustainability |
| **Reserve Fund** | 5–10% | For reversals and unforeseen circumstances |

**Note:** These percentages are suggestions. Final shares must be negotiated with communities and government.

## 5. Community Benefit Sharing Agreement

### 5.1 Agreement Content

| **Element** | **Description** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Preamble** | Background and purpose of agreement |
| **Definitions** | Key terms and concepts |
| **Beneficiaries** | Who benefits and how they are identified |
| **Benefits** | What benefits will be provided |
| **Distribution** | How benefits will be distributed |
| **Governance** | Who manages benefit distribution |
| **Monitoring** | How benefit sharing will be monitored |
| **Dispute Resolution** | How disputes will be resolved |
| **Duration** | How long the agreement is valid |
| **Signatures** | Signatures of all parties |

### 5.2 Agreement Requirements

- Must be written in **local languages**
- Must be reviewed with community members
- Must be signed by community representatives
- Must be signed by traditional authorities
- Must be signed by project representative
- Must be witnessed by independent observer

## 6. Governance Structure

### 6.1 Benefit Sharing Committee

| **Role** | **Responsibilities** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Community Representatives** | Represent community interests; approve benefit distribution |
| **Traditional Authorities** | Provide traditional leadership; support community decisions |
| **Project Representative** | Facilitate benefit distribution; provide project information |
| **Women's Representative** | Ensure women's inclusion and benefit |
| **Youth Representative** | Ensure youth inclusion and benefit |
| **Independent Observer** | Monitor benefit sharing; ensure fairness and transparency |

### 6.2 Committee Functions

- Monitor benefit distribution
- Address grievances related to benefit sharing
- Report to communities on benefit sharing
- Ensure transparency and accountability

## 8. Grievance Mechanism for Benefit Sharing

### 8.1 Process

| **Level** | **Process** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1: Community** | Individual raises concern with Benefit Sharing Committee |
| **Level 2: Project Management** | Concern escalated to Project Director |
| **Level 3: Mediation** | Independent third-party mediation |
| **Level 4: Legal** | Legal resolution (if necessary) |

### 8.2 Requirements

- **Accessible** — all community members must be able to access the mechanism
- **Free** — no cost to raise concerns
- **Confidential** — privacy of complainants protected
- **Timely** — concerns addressed within reasonable timeframes
- **Transparent** — process and outcomes documented

## 10. Key Reminders for Field Team

1. **Benefits must be tangible** — communities must see and feel the benefits
2. **Benefits must be transparent** — all benefit sharing must be publicly documented
3. **Communities must participate** — benefit sharing must be community-driven
4. **Disputes must be resolved** — have a clear grievance mechanism
5. **Monitoring must be participatory** — communities must monitor benefit sharing
6. **Documentation is essential** — all benefit sharing must be documented
